#### c. Entity Descriptions

> Source: 94 tables across the 10 business schemas of the live Supabase database (`users_module`, `course_module`, `language_module`, `practice_module`, `flashcard_module`, `exam_module`, `jlpt_module`, `dictionary_module`, `billing_module`, `ai_module`).
> `auth.users` is the Supabase Auth identity table and is not counted as a business entity; columns pointing to it are marked as *Foreign Key to auth.users*.
> Columns marked *(no DB constraint)* reference another entity logically but have no database-level foreign key (polymorphic or cross-schema references).
